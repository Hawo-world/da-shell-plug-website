About Me

Aloha! my name is Chelsea McKenzie. Born on the Big Island, raised on the beautiful Garden Isle of Kaua’i, Hawai’i. My Husband and I moved to San Antonio to pursue new opportunities as our family grows.

Though I’m far now, crafting accessories through Da Shell Plug is my way of staying connected to my home. With my passion for authentic Hawaiian seashells, every shell I present is thoughtfully hand-picked from the prestine shores of Kaua'i.

When you support my business, you’re supporting my ʻOhana. Mahalo for being part of our journey and sharing my love for the ʻĀina.

Much Love,
The McKenzies.
